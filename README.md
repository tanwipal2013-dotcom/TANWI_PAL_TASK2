ORDERS AND CUSTOMERS DASHBOARD
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY 
This report provides a professional-level view of sales performance, focusing on profit margin, 
region, product category, regions, segment, and discount impact. It is designed to support data
driven decisions and is directly adapted to a Power BI sales dashboard.  
Sales Performance Analysis 
• Sales Performance: Calculate total sales performance of all regions. 
• Highest Revenue Region: Identify highest revenue across regions by finding segment and     
product category . 
•Highest Profit Margin: Identify total profit margin, estimate its profit margin % across category. 
•Average Order Value: Calculate average order value of products. 
• Customer Contribution: Identify the top 10 customers by sales and profit to strengthen 
retention and account-management strategies. 
Profitability & Discount Analysis 
Monitor month wise discount and analyse its effects on product profit. A discount may increase 
sales volume while reducing the profit earned per order. Here a diagram is showed where 
discount band and Business decision has discussed. 
Key Recommendations  
1. Focus on profitable growth: Prioritize regions and products that generate both strong sales 
and strong profit.  
2. Control discounting: Review high-discount transactions and establish discount thresholds 
based on margin. 
3. Monitor sales trends: Use monthly performance tracking to identify changes across regions 
and product Performance. 
4.Region Wise Product Demand: Monitor region wise product demand, profit margin, discount 
band  respectively.
