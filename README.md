EXECUTIVE SUMMARY 
This Sales Performance Analysis report delivers a high-level review of key revenue drivers, focusing on profit margins, regional trends, product categories, customer segments, and discount impacts. Designed to support data-driven decision-making, it integrates seamlessly with our Power BI sales dashboard.  
 
Sales Performance Analysis
• Sales Performance: Evaluate aggregate sales volume and revenue across all operating regions.
• Top-Performing Regions: Identify the highest-revenue geographic markets, segmented by customer type and product category.
• Profitability Optimization: Determine net profit margins and calculate exact profit margin percentages across each product category. 
• Highest Profit Margin: Identify total profit margin, estimate its profit margin % across category. 
• Average Order Value: Calculate average order value of products. 
• Customer Contribution: Identify the top 10 customers by sales and profit to strengthen 
retention and account-management strategies. 
Profitability & Discount Analysis 

Monitor monthly promotional strategies to analyze the direct impact of discount structures on product profitability. While discounting can accelerate sales volume, it frequently erodes per-order margins. The accompanying diagram illustrates how specific discount bands intersect with corporate margin targets to guide strategic business decisions.

1. Prioritize Profitable Growth: Focus resources on the specific regions and product lines that generate high concurrent sales volume and strong profit margins.
2. Optimize Discounting Frameworks: Review high-discount transactions and establish strict discount thresholds based on baseline product margins.
